Engineered For Student Governance

Everything Your Council Needs to Govern

Tailored to the unique processes of university councils—eliminating untracked paperwork, lost deliverables, and unclear accountability.

Kanban, List & Calendar Views

Track tasks across real institutional stages: Not Started, Processing, To Advisers, Campus Office routing (OCA, OSAS, Accounting, PPSS), and Completed.

Multi-Tenant Workspaces

Independent, secure workspaces for CSG, CO:DE, ACES, and college councils with customizable theme color palettes, official logos, and position titles.

Org Admin Password Security

Sensitive actions—marking a task completed or deleting tasks—strictly require Org Admin password authorization to guarantee verified accountability.

Adviser Review & Executive Control

Faculty advisers review proof of work documents, while Student Council Presidents possess executive task reassignments and stage override privileges.

Instant PDF & Excel Reporting

Generate printable, auditable PDF summaries and spreadsheet data exports of council performance, overdue metrics, and committee task completion rates.

In-App Alerts & Deadline Nudges

Automated alerts, overdue flags, and priority-tier badges (Low, Medium, High, Urgent) ensure deliverables are never neglected before deadlines.

Institutional Process

How Council Workflows Move

A four-step lifecycle ensuring full transparency, accountability, and institutional continuity.

01

Register & Onboard

Submit your student organization registration. Once verified and approved by the Super Admin, the Org Admin configures positions, titles, and officers.

02

Delegate & Prioritize

Presidents and Admins create tasks with specific categories (Document, Event, Finance, Multimedia), set priority tiers (Low to Urgent), and assign officers.

03

Execute & Track

Officers update progress sliders, log remarks, attach proof-of-work documentation, and advance tasks across the interactive Kanban board.

04

Authorize & Archive

Tasks routed to Advisers receive faculty review. Final completion requires Org Admin password verification, permanently logging into the activity audit trail.

Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers regarding access, council registration, permissions, and security.

Any recognized campus council or organization can register via the Register Organization portal. Once submitted, the CSG Super Admin reviews and verifies the application. Upon approval, the designated Org Admin receives access to configure positions, invite officers, and start delegating initiatives.
The platform enforces strict Role-Based Access Control (RBAC):
  • Super Admin: Global oversight, multi-tenant workspace switching, organization approvals, and activity logs.
  • Org Admin: Organization manager. Only the Org Admin's password can authorize task completion and deletion.
  • President: Executive organizational leader. Can manage officers, create tasks, and reassign/override any initiative.
  • Elected Officer & Committee Member: Execute assigned tasks, update progress sliders, and attach proof-of-work.
  • Adviser: Faculty review and verification of completed milestones.
To prevent accidental loss of records and ensure verified accountability across student government, sensitive actions (marking complete or deleting) require the Org Admin's password authorization. This guarantees that all finished initiatives are genuinely audited.
Yes. The platform uses a multi-tenant database design. Tasks, officer rosters, reports, remarks, and documents are strictly scoped to your specific organization workspace.
